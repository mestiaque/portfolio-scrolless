1. Seed → Tree → Digital World

Create a cinematic, photorealistic interactive portfolio hero concept for a creative developer.

Story progression driven entirely by vertical scrolling:

Scene 1: Extreme macro shot of a tiny realistic seed buried beneath dark, moist forest soil. Subtle particles, roots, moisture, natural light rays.

Scene 2: As the user scrolls, the seed slowly cracks and a tiny green sprout emerges from the soil.

Scene 3: Continued scrolling causes the plant to naturally grow into a young tree. Roots expand underground while branches and leaves grow organically.

Scene 4: The tree becomes large and mature inside a realistic forest. Wind gently moves the leaves, sunlight changes naturally.

Scene 5: Camera slowly moves toward a single leaf. A tiny water droplet sits on the leaf.

Scene 6: The camera pushes extremely close into the water droplet. Inside the reflection, a futuristic digital world begins to appear.

Scene 7: The natural forest seamlessly transforms into a futuristic digital landscape made of glowing particles, networks, code fragments and geometric structures.

Scene 8: Camera travels through this digital world and eventually transitions into the developer portfolio.

Visual direction: extremely realistic nature, cinematic depth of field, physically accurate lighting, subtle atmospheric particles, realistic wind and motion, premium film production quality, no cartoon aesthetic, no exaggerated sci-fi.

The entire experience should feel like a continuous unbroken cinematic shot controlled by scroll.

Three.js-friendly: use procedural particles, shaders, instancing, camera interpolation, morphing geometry, depth effects and WebGL-friendly assets.

Mood: mysterious, elegant, organic, technological.

16:9 cinematic composition.
